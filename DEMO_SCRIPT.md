# Demo Video Script — Mushak Yatra (2 minutes)

A contest demo should show the game **from start to finish**. Keep it under 3 minutes.

## Before recording

1. Run `npm run dev` (or open your live link) and play a few rounds to warm up.
2. **Reset the tutorial** so the hints appear in the video: open the browser console and run
   `__game.save.tutorialDone = false; __game.writeSave();`
3. Turn the **sound on** — the dhol music is a big part of the impression.
4. Record in **landscape on a laptop** (clearer for judges), or portrait on a phone if you prefer.
5. Close other tabs so the frame rate stays high.
6. Recording tools: Xbox Game Bar (`Win + G`) on Windows, or OBS Studio.

## The script

### 0:00–0:12 — Title and the hook
**Show:** the title screen with Mushak running behind it.

> "Ganpati Bappa Morya! This is **Mushak Yatra — The Road to Bappa**, a 3D Ganesh Chaturthi game
> that runs in any browser, on phones and laptops. You play Mushak, Ganesha's mouse, bringing
> offerings to Bappa before the aarti."

### 0:12–0:30 — Starting, and how easy it is to pick up
**Show:** press Start. The first obstacle appears, time slows, the hint shows "Press ↑ to jump".
Jump over it. Then the slide hint, then the lane-change hint.

> "A new player learns in seconds — the first time each obstacle appears, time slows down and the
> game shows you exactly what to do."

### 0:30–0:50 — Collecting and the Dhol Beat
**Show:** collect a modak trail, watch the Modak meter fill, jump in time with the beat so the
combo counter climbs to x2 or x3.

> "Collect modaks and offerings. The whole festival moves to the dhol beat — move in time with it
> and your score multiplier grows, up to five times."

### 0:50–1:05 — ⭐ Vighnaharta Mode (the highlight)
**Show:** reach 21 modaks so the mode triggers. Run straight at obstacles and let them burst into
marigold petals.

> "Collect 21 modaks, the traditional offering, and Vighnaharta Mode begins. Ganesha is the
> remover of obstacles — so every obstacle in front of Mushak turns into flower petals."

*Let this run a few seconds with the sound up. It is the most memorable part.*

### 1:05–1:15 — Eco Gates and the story quiz
**Show:** run through a green clay gate, then the story card and quiz between acts, and answer it.

> "Choosing a clay murti over plastic earns a Green Blessing. Between acts there's a short story
> about Ganesha and a quick question."

### 1:15–1:35 — Acts 2 and 3
**Show:** the pandal street at night with the lights pulsing on the beat, then the sea promenade
with the moon, floating diyas and fireworks.

> "The journey has three acts — the morning bazaar, the pandal at night, and the Visarjan
> procession by the sea."

### 1:35–1:55 — The finale and results
**Show:** the shrine arriving, the diyas lighting one by one, fireworks, then the results screen
with the stars and the score.

> "Reach the shore and the aarti begins, with the offerings you collected. You get a score and up
> to three stars, which unlock Endless Mode."

### 1:55–2:05 — Close
**Show:** the results screen, or the title screen with your best score.

> "Everything in the game — every model, texture and sound, including the dhol music — is
> generated in code, so the whole game is about 160 KB and loads instantly. Built with Three.js.
> Ganpati Bappa Morya!"

## Tips

- **Don't narrate every detail.** Let the game be seen; judges mostly watch.
- **Show a complete loop:** start → play → result → play again. The contest asks for it.
- If you lose all 3 diyas mid-recording, keep going and show the results screen anyway — then
  restart. It proves the full flow works.
- Mention the phone support by **showing** it: a few seconds of portrait phone play is convincing.
- Keep the music audible but talk over it, not under it.

## One-line description (for the submission form)

> Mushak Yatra is a 3D Ganesh Chaturthi runner where you play Mushak, Ganesha's mouse, gathering
> modaks and offerings across three festival acts — the dawn bazaar, the night pandal and the
> Visarjan by the sea — collecting 21 modaks to trigger Vighnaharta Mode, which turns every
> obstacle into flower petals. It runs in any browser on phone or laptop, and every model,
> texture and sound is generated in code.
